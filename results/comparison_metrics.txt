Neural Network (Intrinsic MLP)
------------------------------
Command: python src/neural_network_model.py
Key adjustments: re-enabled location-aware one-hot encodings (each capped at the 50 most frequent categories), widened the MLP stack with dropout (0.25), used AdamW (lr=5e-4, weight decay=5e-4) under an 8-epoch warmup + cosine decay schedule, introduced price-weighted mini-batches, and activated early stopping with patience 20. The pipeline now consumes the full 100,000 cleaned rows instead of subsampling so the neural net trains on the same coverage as the XGBoost and GLM baselines.

Training behaviour snapshot:
  - Training RMSE settled near 0.200 in log $/sqft, while validation RMSE fluctuated between 0.29 and 0.33 before patience triggered at epoch 143.
  - Using every row improves stability versus the earlier 50k subsample runs and removes variance caused by random sampling.
  - The expanded architecture and schedule increased per-epoch cost but produced smoother convergence.

Evaluation snapshot (best checkpoint → best_intrinsic_mlp.pt):
  - Dollar space: RMSE $114.87 | MAE $45.81 | <5% accuracy 20.27% | <15% accuracy 57.32%
  - Log space: RMSE 0.2891 | MAE 0.1827
  - Historical targets: WITHOUT location → <5% 24.60%, <15% 64.54%; WITH location → <5% 27.43%, <15% 70.10%. Despite richer features, the run still trails by 4.3–12.8 percentage points.

Interpretation & diagnostics:
  - Full-dataset training plus price-weighted loss reduces dollar RMSE from ~$389 to ~$115 and drops log RMSE from 0.3493 to 0.2891 compared with the previous numeric-only run, showing the upgrades helped calibration.
  - Percentage accuracy remains below target because residual variance in high-price metros is still large; location one-hots alone are not capturing fine-grained neighbourhood effects.
  - Training and validation curves stay relatively close, implying the model is now capacity-limited rather than overfitting; larger hidden widths or learned embeddings should be explored next.

Next steps:
  1. Replace one-hots with learnable ZIP/CITY embeddings to capture neighbourhood structure with fewer parameters.
  2. Engineer interaction features (e.g., SQFT × HOTNESS_SCORE, PROPERTY_AGE buckets) via the shared preprocessor so tree and GLM baselines benefit in tandem.
  3. Experiment with deeper or residual MLP stacks while preserving dropout/weight decay to lift representational capacity.
  4. Incorporate LR restarts or ReduceLROnPlateau once validation RMSE stalls to extract more from later epochs.
  5. Produce decile-wise calibration/error plots to identify which price bands drive the <5%/<15% shortfall and tune loss weights accordingly.

Outcome summary: Training on the entire dataset with the enhanced regimen materially lowered RMSE, yet both <5% and <15% accuracy goals remain unmet. Closing that gap now depends on stronger location representations and targeted feature interactions rather than additional subsampling.

Notes
-----
- All runs apply a log1p transform to the PRICE target during training where indicated, with metrics reported in both the original and transformed spaces.
- The GLM runner defaults to the compact feature set; pass --one-hot to include expanded categorical encodings if memory allows.
- The neural network logs indicate that despite improved RMSE, the validation accuracy still trails historical baselines; richer geospatial features and targeted loss weighting remain the top levers.
