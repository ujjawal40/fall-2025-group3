4. SUMMARY (H1 vs H2)
---------------------

- H2 (2-month ahead) generally achieves:
    * Lower MAE and WAPE
    * Better median %-error (MdAPE)
    * Higher fraction of predictions within ±10% of true prices
    * Narrower prediction intervals (lower relative width)

- H1 (1-month ahead) is slightly more conservative:
    * Very strong R² and WAPE
    * Higher PI coverage and somewhat wider intervals
    * Heavier suppression (more predictions gated out) under the width threshold

Overall, both horizons show very strong generalization on both the fixed holdout
and the rolling backtests, with H2 being sharper and H1 being slightly more
conservative in its uncertainty quantification.
